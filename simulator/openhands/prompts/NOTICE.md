# Upstream prompt provenance

`tom_swe_user.json` selects exact sentences from the pinned sources linked in that
file. It does not reimplement FakeUser or claim to reproduce Stateful SWE.
The OpenHands source is MIT licensed (notice below). ToM-SWE declares MIT in its
`pyproject.toml`; the single ongoing-clarification sentence is retained verbatim.
Local language, workspace and control-tool instructions are integration glue,
not attributed to the paper. No upstream fixed reply, turn cap, refusal persona,
random preference generation or history rewriting is enabled.

Reference: Zhou et al., *ToM-SWE: User Mental Modeling for Software Engineering
Agents*, arXiv:2510.21903v2, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21903v2.
